About Mountain Creek Brewing Co.

Brewed Here. Finished by You.

Our Story

Mountain Creek Brewing Co. was created for people who love great beer but don't always have the time to spend an entire day brewing. We believe making exceptional craft beer should be enjoyable, not complicated. That's why we professionally brew every batch of wort, allowing you to experience the rewarding part of brewing—fermentation, packaging and sharing great beer.

Why Choose Fresh Wort?

Commercial Brewery Quality

Every batch is brewed on our commercial brewery using proven brewing practices and premium ingredients.

Fresh Every Time

We brew in small batches so every Fresh Wort Kit is delivered at peak freshness and ready to ferment.

Simple Brewing

Transfer the wort to your fermenter, pitch your yeast and let fermentation do the rest.

Our Brewing Philosophy

We don't believe in shortcuts when it comes to brewing. Every recipe has been carefully developed using quality Australian malts, premium hops and carefully selected yeast strains to produce beers we're proud to put our name on. Our goal isn't simply to sell wort kits—it's to help home brewers produce commercial-quality beer with confidence.

Proudly Sunshine Coast

Located on Queensland's Sunshine Coast, Mountain Creek Brewing Co. is a small independent brewery focused on quality over quantity. By brewing in smaller batches, we're able to maintain consistency, freshness and attention to detail in every wort kit we produce.

Ready to Brew?

Whether you're brewing your first beer or your hundredth, our Premium Fresh Wort Kits provide the perfect starting point for exceptional craft beer at home.

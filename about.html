Our Story

Mountain Creek Brewing Co. was founded with one simple mission — to help home brewers create exceptional beer without spending an entire day brewing. We professionally brew every batch of fresh wort using commercial brewing equipment and premium ingredients. You simply ferment, package and enjoy. Whether it's your first brew or your hundredth, our goal is to give you the best possible start to making outstanding beer.

Why Choose Mountain Creek Brewing Co.?

🍺 Commercial Brewery Quality

Every wort kit is brewed on professional brewing equipment using proven brewing techniques.

🌿 Premium Ingredients

We use quality Australian and international malts together with carefully selected hops and yeast.

⚙️ Brew Without the Brew Day

No mashing. No boiling. No complicated equipment. Simply ferment, package and enjoy.

Fresh Wort. Fresh Beer.

Fresh wort provides the perfect starting point for brewing great beer. Every batch is brewed fresh in small quantities before being packaged ready for fermentation. That means you receive commercial-quality wort without the time, mess and complexity of a traditional brew day.

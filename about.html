Our Story

Mountain Creek Brewing Co. was founded with a simple goal — to make brewing exceptional craft beer easier for everyone. Whether you're brewing your very first batch or you've been home brewing for years, we believe your time should be spent enjoying the rewarding parts of brewing rather than standing over a kettle all day.

Every Premium Fresh Wort Kit is brewed on commercial equipment using premium ingredients and proven brewing techniques. We take care of the mash and boil, leaving you to ferment, package and enjoy great beer.

🍺 Commercial Brewery Quality

Our wort is brewed on professional brewing equipment using carefully developed recipes and quality ingredients.

🌿 Premium Ingredients

Quality Australian and imported malts combine with carefully selected hops to create beers full of flavour and character.

⚙️ Brew Without the Brew Day

No mashing. No boiling. No complicated equipment. Simply transfer the wort, pitch your yeast and let fermentation do the rest.

Why Fresh Wort?

Fresh wort gives you the opportunity to produce commercial-quality beer at home without spending an entire day brewing. Our wort kits are supplied fresh and ready for fermentation, giving you complete control over the final stages of brewing while saving hours of work.

✔ Small Batch Brewing

We brew in small batches to ensure consistency, freshness and attention to detail.

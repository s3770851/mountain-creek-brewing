Mountain IPA

American IPA • 22L Fresh Wort Kit

Brewing Guide

Congratulations on choosing Mountain IPA. This fresh wort kit has been professionally brewed using premium Australian malt and carefully selected hops. All that's left is to ferment and package your beer.

Recommended Yeast

Fermentis US-05

Pitch yeast between 18–20°C.


Dry Hop Schedule

Day 4 50g Galaxy
Day 5 50g Citra
Day 6 50g Simcoe

Fermentation Schedule

  • Pitch yeast at 18–20°C
  • Maintain fermentation at 18°C
  • Once final gravity is almost reached, raise temperature to 20–21°C
  • Hold for 2 days for a diacetyl rest
  • Cold crash if desired
  • Package once gravity readings remain stable over 2 consecutive days

Packaging

Once gravity has remained stable for two days, your beer is ready to bottle or keg. For best results allow adequate conditioning before serving. Enjoy fresh craft beer... Brewed Here. Finished by You.

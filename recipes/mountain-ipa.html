Fermentation Guide

  • Pitch yeast between 18–20°C.
  • Maintain fermentation at 18°C.
  • Dry hop on Days 4, 5 and 6.
  • Once fermentation is almost complete, raise to 20–21°C for a 2-day diacetyl rest.
  • Cold crash if desired.
  • Package only after gravity has remained stable over two consecutive days.

Troubleshooting

Airlock isn't bubbling?

Don't panic. Fermentation may already be underway. Always check gravity before assuming fermentation has stalled.

Fermentation is slow

Keep the fermenter at approximately 18°C. US-05 performs best with stable temperatures.

Can I cold crash?

Yes. Once gravity has been stable for two consecutive days.

Can I leave hops longer?

Yes. Leaving hops an extra day or two is perfectly acceptable.

Packaging

Bottle or keg once gravity has remained stable for two consecutive days.

Recommended carbonation: 2.4–2.5 volumes of CO₂

Brewmaster's Notes

Mountain IPA is designed to showcase bold American hop character balanced by a clean malt backbone.

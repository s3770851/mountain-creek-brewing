About Mountain Creek Brewing Co.

Brewed Here. Finished by You.

Our Story

Mountain Creek Brewing Co. was founded with one simple goal — to make commercial-quality craft beer accessible to everyone.

Not everyone has the time, equipment or experience to spend an entire day brewing, but everyone deserves the opportunity to create exceptional beer at home.

That's why we do the hard work for you. Every batch is professionally brewed using premium Australian and international ingredients, allowing you to focus on fermentation, packaging and enjoying great beer with family and friends.

What Makes Us Different?

Commercial Equipment

Every wort kit is brewed on our commercial brewery system using professional brewing techniques.

Small Batch Brewing

We brew in small batches to ensure every customer receives the freshest wort possible.

Premium Ingredients

Quality malt, premium hops and carefully selected yeast create beers you'll be proud to share.

Fresh Wort Kits

Our Premium Fresh Wort Kits arrive ready for fermentation. Simply transfer to your fermenter, pitch your yeast and let nature do the rest.

No mashing. No boiling. No complicated brew day. Just fresh commercial-quality wort waiting to become exceptional beer.

Proudly Sunshine Coast

Based on Queensland's beautiful Sunshine Coast, Mountain Creek Brewing Co. proudly brews premium fresh wort for home brewers who appreciate quality, consistency and flavour.

Whether you're brewing your very first batch or your hundredth, our goal is simple— help you create beer you'll be proud to pour.
